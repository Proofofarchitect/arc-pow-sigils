## The 20-gwei rule

Arc silently drops any transaction with `maxFeePerGas` below **20 gwei**. Always set at least 20 gwei. If a transaction seems to "disappear", this is the most common cause.

## We will never ask for secrets

Nobody from the project will ever:

- DM you first.
- Ask for your seed phrase or private key.
- Ask you to "verify" your wallet by entering a secret anywhere.

If someone does any of these, they are impersonating the project. Do not engage.

## Treat claim codes as secrets

Unclaimed claim codes can be claimed by **anyone** who holds them. If a code leaks before it is used, a stranger can spend it. Keep your codes private until you have claimed — see [Claim Codes](claim-codes.md).

## How to stay safe

- Bookmark the official website and use that link every time.
- Verify the contract address on the explorer before minting.
- Set `maxFeePerGas` to at least 20 gwei.
- Remember that gas is paid in native USDC, not ETH.
- Trust nothing that is not published on the official website.
